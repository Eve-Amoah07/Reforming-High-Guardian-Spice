Back in February of 2020, Crunchyroll, an American subscription-based anime streaming service based in San Francisco, California, and owned by Sony Group Corporation, announced to its user base that they would be funding, producing, and distributing their own original anime content for the platform in collaboration with a variety of other animation studios and creators in order to bring people exclusive and special content to both their subscribers and the anime community as a whole.

Their main goal in initiating this was to expand their catalogue and engage with new and established creators to develop high-quality content during the significant growth and rise of streaming services such as Netflix and HBO Max.

One of the many original works amongst the 'Crunchyroll Originals' label, High Guardian Spice was one of the streaming service's first shows to ever be picked up, having been conceptualized and pitched to the company by Raye Rodriguez, a Cuban-American transgender man.

Created in an in-house studio known as Crunchyroll Studios—formerly Ellation Studios, it was slated to be the first series to be aired under the company. After trial and tribulations—and a lot of internet criticism—that caused the show to be delayed on the back-burner for two consecutive years, High Guardian Spice's one and only season, consisting of 12 20-minute episodes, was fully released to the public on the 26th of October back in 2021.

Despite being in production hell for such a period of time, the outcome was unfortunately nothing short of a muddled disaster. Filled with uncertainty and confusion, it gained mixed—though mostly negative—reception online, as the internet and critics alike picked the show apart. Even after the year of the show's release, its mark on the inter-webs still stands, as folks can only look on and wonder what went wrong.
