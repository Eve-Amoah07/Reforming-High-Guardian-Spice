Hi, creator speaking here. Thanks for taking the time to check out the site! This project has been in the works for a long time, and it still has quite a way to go before it reaches completion.

I am not creating this reformation project out of spite, pettiness, or ill-will. This site's sole purpose is to be a learning experience and a creative outlet for writing and art. It's an attempt to reform a show I believe had genuine, yet squandered potential.

This site contains content that may reference persons or companies involved in High Guardian Spice. I do not condone harassment or hate toward anyone mentioned. This project is about creativity and growth, not negativity.

I commend Raye Rodriguez for getting his passion project animated — a dream I also hope to chase someday. Everyone has their own tastes, and I respect those who enjoyed the show. Constructive criticism is welcome!

Thank you for reading. Happy browsing!

Back in February 2020, Crunchyroll announced its initiative to produce original anime content. Among these was High Guardian Spice, created by Raye Rodriguez. Despite delays and criticism, the show aired in October 2021.

Unfortunately, the reception was largely negative. This project seeks to explore what went wrong and reimagine the series with new ideas, writing, and art.
