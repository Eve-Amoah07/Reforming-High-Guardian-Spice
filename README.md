# Reforming-High-Guardian-Spice
Reforming High Guardian Spice is a creative overhaul of the original series by an aspiring writer/artist/animator. This work-in-progress project reimagines the show’s plot, characters, and lore through original concepts, art, and writing—aiming to revive its potential and transform its legacy.

⚠️ *AUTHOR'S NOTE! –– THIS SITE IS AN INCOMPLETE WORK IN PROGRESS, EXPECT MISSING ELEMENTS AND POTENTIAL BUGS ⚠️
This site serves to be a collective of the author's ideas, concepts, artwork, and creativity in order to make attempts fix issues within the show's plot, writing, characters and character development, and surrounding lore within the fictional world. It has long been in the works since the show's release, as the author continues to make plans to alter the series into something worthwhile.
